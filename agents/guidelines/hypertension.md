# Hypertension (illustrative summary, not clinical advice)
Office systolic blood pressure persistently at or above 140 mmHg suggests uncontrolled hypertension.
Review adherence and home readings before intensifying therapy.
Calcium channel blockers such as amlodipine are metabolised by CYP3A4; strong CYP3A4 inhibitors can raise their levels and cause edema or hypotension.
